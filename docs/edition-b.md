## Current valuation, disagreement and reversal evidence

The operating DCF range is **$2.68–$6.11**, with a **$4.49 base**, versus **$13.03 at 11:15 a.m. EDT on October 19**. This is a frozen intraday snapshot, not a live quote at recording time. PEGA's EV/revenue reference implies **$12.75** and its reported-P/E reference **$8.39**. The sales reference is counterevidence to claiming every method shows overvaluation. The methods are not averaged; the recommendation depends on the student's conservative view of sustainable operating cash profitability.

Reconsider the forecast if ARR growth exceeds 12% for two quarters, retention reaches 110%, agentic ARR is demonstrably incremental, GAAP gross margin stabilizes near 81%, and sales/marketing grows slower than gross profit. Retention below 105% for two quarters strengthens the downside. Thresholds prompt a new forecast and valuation; none automatically authorizes a purchase.

The main limitations are the assumed 11% WACC, approximately 79% terminal share of base enterprise value, lagged opening balances, basic fixed shares with an economic SBC-cost approximation, and one-peer evidence. All current operating cases preserve non-initiation; the downside test did not reverse the action. These qualifications constrain confidence in precise endpoints.

**What changed and why:** the investment question moved from whether UiPath can participate in agentic automation to whether it can do so incrementally and profitably.
