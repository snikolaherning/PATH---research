# UiPath, Inc. (NYSE: PATH) — Company Research Report

## Investment View

My judgment is that UiPath has a strong product positioned for demand in intelligent and agentic automation, but the strength and durability of that demand have not yet been independently established in this report. **[Source: UNSOURCED for “strong product” and market-demand judgment; analyst judgment · call date September 3, 2026. Management’s description of its agentic-automation positioning: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · p. 59 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

I believe the company could benefit from its platform and operating scale as it shifts from rule-based automation toward AI-enabled agents, but I am not ready to initiate a position before completing the valuation and resolving the weaker customer-expansion signals. **[Source: analyst judgment · call date September 3, 2026. Platform transition: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · p. 59 · filed March 25, 2026. Customer-expansion indicators: Item 7 · pp. 67–68.]**

## Company and Filing

This report primarily uses UiPath’s Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended January 31, 2026, accession number 0001734722-26-000012. **[Source: SEC EDGAR filing index · filed March 25, 2026 · period of report January 31, 2026.]**

## What the Business Sells

UiPath describes its platform as an enterprise-automation system evolving from rule-based automation toward intelligent and agentic automation. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · p. 59 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

The company generates revenue from software licenses and related maintenance and support, access to cloud-hosted SaaS products, and professional services. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · pp. 60–61 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

License revenue is generally recognized when customers can use the software, while maintenance, support, and SaaS revenue are recognized over the contract term; professional-services revenue is recognized as services are delivered. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · pp. 60–61 and Critical Accounting Estimates · pp. 71–72 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

Professional services include agentic-automation deployment, customer education, and training. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · p. 61 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

## Recent Performance Against Its Own History

Fiscal 2026 revenue increased 13% to $1.611 billion from $1.430 billion in fiscal 2025, with subscription-services revenue increasing 19% to $954.5 million. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · pp. 64–65 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

Management attributed 85% of total revenue growth to existing customers and 15% to new customers. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · p. 65 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

ARR increased 11% to $1.853 billion from $1.666 billion, but incremental ARR declined to $186.4 million from $202.4 million and dollar-based net retention declined to 107% from 110%. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · Key Performance Metric · pp. 67–68 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

Gross margin remained 83%, while operating results improved to $56.8 million of income from a $162.6 million loss. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · pp. 64–65 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

Operating cash flow increased to $371.2 million from $320.6 million. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · Cash Flows · pp. 69–70 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

Fiscal 2026 net income was $282.3 million and included a $181.7 million income-tax benefit, which management said was driven mainly by releasing a valuation allowance associated with its U.S. entity and by changes in the geographic mix of operating profits. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · pp. 64 and 67 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

## What Is Good and What Is Bad

The positive evidence is the combination of double-digit revenue and ARR growth, an operating-profit inflection, stable gross margin, and higher operating cash flow. **[Source: analyst synthesis · call date September 3, 2026; underlying figures: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · pp. 60 and 64–70 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

The negative evidence is that dollar-based net retention and incremental ARR weakened, professional-services cost increased 53% to $108.1 million, and headline net income benefited substantially from the tax-valuation-allowance release. **[Source: analyst synthesis · call date September 3, 2026; underlying figures: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · pp. 65–68 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

Management attributed the $37.3 million increase in professional-services cost primarily to a $33.5 million increase in third-party subcontractor costs. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · p. 65 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

My judgment is that the improvement in operating income is meaningful, but the lower retention rate and tax-driven portion of net income justify waiting for more evidence before initiating a position. **[Source: analyst judgment · call date September 3, 2026; underlying figures: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · pp. 64 and 67–68 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

## Advantages, Challenges, and Risks

Management presents UiPath’s automation experience, product integrations, security, governance, and enterprise-scale positioning as advantages in the transition toward agentic automation. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · p. 59 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

My judgment is that the existing platform and recurring customer base could provide scale advantages if customers adopt the expanded agentic-automation offering. **[Source: analyst judgment · call date September 3, 2026; evidence that the platform or customer base will create a durable competitive advantage: UNSOURCED.]**

Management cautions that ARR is not GAAP revenue, is not a forecast, may fluctuate with customer satisfaction, pricing, competition, economic conditions, customer spending, contraction, and cancellations, and may not be comparable with similarly titled metrics from other companies. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · pp. 67–68 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

Management expects cloud-hosting costs to rise as SaaS adoption expands and states that this shift could affect gross margin. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · p. 61 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

Approximately 51% of fiscal 2026 revenue and 41% of expenses were denominated in non-U.S. currencies, and UiPath recognized $7.0 million of net foreign-currency transaction losses. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7A · p. 73 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

Management estimated that a hypothetical 10% exchange-rate change would have produced a $29.6 million translation effect. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7A · p. 73 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

UiPath reported $871.2 million of cash and equivalents and $818.3 million of marketable securities exposed to interest-rate movements, but management estimated that a hypothetical 10% interest-rate change would not have materially affected fiscal 2026 financial statements. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7A · p. 73 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

## Outlook

Management did not provide a precise numerical revenue or earnings forecast in Item 7. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · pp. 59–72 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

Management expects research-and-development expense and professional-services cost to increase in absolute dollars, while expanded SaaS deployment could increase cloud-infrastructure costs and affect gross margin. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · pp. 61–62 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

Management stated that existing cash, marketable securities, and operating cash flow should be sufficient for anticipated requirements over the next twelve months and the long term. **[Source: UiPath FY2026 Form 10-K · Item 7 · Liquidity and Capital Resources · p. 69 · filed March 25, 2026.]**

My judgment is that UiPath has sufficient financial capacity to keep investing, but this report does not yet demonstrate that future agentic-automation demand, customer expansion, or margin performance will justify initiating a position at the prevailing valuation. **[Source: analyst judgment · call date September 3, 2026; independent demand and valuation support: UNSOURCED.]**
